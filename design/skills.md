| skill | effect |
|---|---|
| fireball | high-damage spell |
| dodge | avoid incoming hits |

| basic training | bonus attack damage, attack speed, hp and armor |
| frenzy | gain an attack speed bonus |

## Critical hits (mechanic idea, not implemented)

- No randomness: a critical hit comes every few attacks. Critical chance sets
  how often: each attack adds the chance to a counter, and when it reaches
  100% that attack is critical and 100% is taken off (25% = every 4th
  attack; 30% = attacks 4, 7, 10, ...).
- Critical damage: an attribute, starting at 200% (a critical hit deals twice
  a normal hit); it can be increased.
- On-hit damage does not crit by default. Still to decide: spells.
